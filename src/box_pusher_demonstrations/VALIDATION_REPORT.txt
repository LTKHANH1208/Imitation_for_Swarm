IMPORTANT DATA CONTRACT
- Policy baseline observation: left raw RGB resized to 96x72.
- Action: linear.x and angular.z from TwistStamped seen on the controller input
  topic. This is a published command, not a low-level motor-acceptance ACK.
- The latest sensor message at or before action stamp is selected. Future frames
  are never paired with a past action.
- Required left image maximum age: 0.15 s.
- Optional depth maximum age: 0.60 s.
- Ground-truth Gazebo poses, collision events and task evaluator outputs are not
  subscribed to or stored in the demonstration sample.
- Auxiliary V2 channels must not be fed to the paper-style baseline policy by
  default.
- Stop recording before simulation reset; start a new episode after reset.
